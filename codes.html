Codes & Scripts

HUB — Heterogeneous Ice Nucleation

I built two scripts to predict droplet-freezing curves and infer the underlying distribution of nucleation temperatures from experimental data.

AINTBAD & IPA — Homogeneous Ice Nucleation

I co-developed tools to quantify how differences in droplet volume affect estimates of homogeneous ice nucleation rates.

ice-WEDGE — Ice Nucleation in Wedge-Shaped Pores

I used Gaussian process regression to predict ice nucleation temperatures from the angle and depth of a pore.
